On-premises deployment

AI that works inside the hospital.

SCOVION supports on-premises deployment, with AI inference performed on computing resources within the hospital. Patient and clinical data can remain in the institution's controlled environment while teams retain responsive access to AI-assisted results.

For configured on-premises workflows, AI inference is performed locally and patient and clinical data remain within the hospital environment.

Deployment scope and system compatibility are assessed for each hospital.Discuss local deployment
01

Local by design

AI inference runs on hospital-based computing resources, reducing reliance on external cloud processing.

02

Data under hospital governance

Data handling can follow the hospital's own access, retention and security policies within its controlled environment.

03

Responsive at the point of care

Local processing can reduce network round trips and support low-latency access to AI-assisted results.

04

Continuity around local operations

Core inference workflows can remain available during external network interruptions, subject to local infrastructure availability.

05

Planned for the existing environment

Deployment is planned around current imaging and information systems, with local configuration, validation and team training.

Regulatory & market access

Regulatory pathways across our healthcare portfolio

Our healthcare portfolio addresses multiple market-specific regulatory pathways. The applicable mark, clearance or approval depends on the individual product and market.

i

Status varies by product, indication, software version and market. A mark shown for one product does not mean that every product holds the same status.

CEEU

A European conformity marking indicating that the relevant medical device has completed the applicable EU conformity-assessment route.

FDAUS

The U.S. FDA applies risk-based medical-device pathways that may include 510(k) clearance, De Novo classification or PMA approval.

UKCAGB

A Great Britain conformity marking used after a medical device has met the applicable UK requirements and assessment route.

NMPACN

China’s medical-device registration and filing framework, administered by the National Medical Products Administration.

MDAMY

Malaysia’s medical-device registration framework under the Medical Device Act 2012 (Act 737), administered by the Medical Device Authority under the Ministry of Health Malaysia.

PMDAJP

Under Japan’s PMD Act framework, PMDA conducts scientific product reviews and post-market safety activities, while the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare makes applicable approval decisions.
