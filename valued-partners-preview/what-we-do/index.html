ESG × VP

Driving sustainable impact

Strategy that can endure scrutiny.

Our collaboration with Eco Sustainability Group brings together regional business networks and specialist sustainability expertise.

For state governments, the aim is to create practical ESG frameworks for sustainable development and green infrastructure. For private enterprises, it is to embed ESG principles into operations, improve competitiveness and build genuine leadership in their industries.

Together, the partnership also encourages education for a growing community of qualified, ESG-conscious practitioners able to implement and monitor these principles across public and private sectors.

Representative case study

Columbia Asia

US$1.2Breported transaction value · September 2019

Columbia Asia operated 31 healthcare facilities across Vietnam, Malaysia, Indonesia and India.

In 2003, Valued Partners brought in the Employee Provident Fund of Malaysia as a strategic 30% shareholder. Over the next 12 years, the firm spearheaded land and hospital acquisitions, capital raising and further strategic partnerships in India and Indonesia.

In September 2019, 17 Columbia Asia hospitals in Malaysia, Indonesia and Vietnam were sold to Hong Leong Group for a reported US$1.2 billion — a defining example of long-horizon shareholder value creation.
